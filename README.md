# research-toolkit

Guides and templates for doing rigorous, reproducible research with AI tools: sourced, dated, and verifiable at every step.

These come out of my own practice as an anthropologist and UX researcher, and from building the research infrastructure for my book, _Mass Intelligence_. The principle throughout: AI helps find, organize, and check sources; the researcher verifies everything, and nothing is cited to the AI itself.

## Contents

| Guide | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [Local Research Assistant: Setup Guide](guides/local-research-assistant.md) | Run a private research assistant on a Mac with LM Studio, a local model, and web-search connectors, with provenance habits built in |

More to come: a template for structuring long-running research collaborations with Claude (task log, session protocol, and working rules).
